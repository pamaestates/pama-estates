# PAMA Estates — Polish Editorial Standard (draft)

## Purpose

The Polish website must sound originally written in Polish for people considering property in Dubai, not translated mechanically from English. Present PAMA as an experienced, discreet Polish-speaking Dubai adviser.

## Voice

- Use elegant, direct, idiomatic Polish. Avoid calques such as “prywatny dostęp” for “private access”.
- Balance lifestyle appeal with practical financial and property considerations. Do not assume every Polish buyer has the same motivation.
- Prefer precise explanations over hype, false urgency, generic luxury adjectives or guaranteed investment returns.
- Explain local UAE terminology on first use where helpful (e.g. off-plan, DLD, service charges, Oqood).
- Keep community, developer and building proper names unchanged unless an established Polish name exists.
- Use clear Polish calls to action: “Zapytaj o szczegóły”, “Porozmawiajmy”, “Umów prezentację”.
- Retain PAMA’s premium navy/gold identity and engineering-led, evidence-based market positioning.

## Strict factual boundaries

1. Never change or invent price, payment plan, floor, area, view, bedrooms, status, availability, discounts, yield or handover date.
2. Display public numeric facts directly from the existing approved feed. Do not copy factual data into translations.
3. Never feed private owner/client data, internal pricing, unit numbers or permit numbers to a translation provider.
4. Public QR verification, publication eligibility and withdrawal must follow the existing source record.
5. If source facts conflict, flag **NOT VERIFIED** and request correction in the source; translation cannot resolve the discrepancy.

## Approval and synchronization policy

- Price, status and photographs synchronize from the single public record; translations cannot override them.
- New/changed descriptions become drafts, not public copy, until approved.
- Store the source fingerprint and reviewer decision to detect stale translations.
- Preserve approved Polish adaptations when English marketing copy changes; request a targeted editorial review.
- If the latest approved Polish text becomes materially misleading, withhold it and show neutral Polish facts only.
- Blog and SEO pages require a managed bilingual content workflow. Arbitrary new React source pages cannot be translated reliably at runtime.
- External AI translation is **not enabled** in PL-BUILD-01. Choose provider, cost controls, privacy assessment and human approval UI separately.

## Acceptance samples

- English: “Private Access. Strategic Advice. Dubai Real Estate.”
- Unacceptable literal: “Prywatny dostęp. Strategiczne doradztwo. Nieruchomości w Dubaju.”
- Preferred adaptation: “Nieruchomości w Dubaju. Sprawdzone możliwości. Świadome decyzje.”

Editorial approval is a separate gate from deployment approval.
